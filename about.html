About the press

Made for the table, rested and ready.

Long Rest Press is a small homebrew label run by a GM who kept asking, "What would this look like at the table?" about every game and book he loved. The answers became subclasses, systems, and adventures for D&D 2024 and Draw Steel.

Everything here is written for play, not just for reading. Each release is versioned, starts as a playtest, and changes as tables report back. If something breaks at your table, that's the most useful thing you can tell us.

All subclasses and systems are free. Our original Draw Steel adventures will be sold separately.

Feedback

Comment on any release thread on Reddit, or use the feedback form (coming soon). Playtest reports with level, party makeup, and what happened are gold.
