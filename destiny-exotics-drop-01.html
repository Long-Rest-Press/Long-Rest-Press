Ghost

Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement), Non-Exotic

A small, floating companion of Light wrapped in a shell. Endlessly curious, occasionally sarcastic, and loyal to the end. Ordinary familiars mostly scout and Help; a Ghost scans, translates, opens doors, carries your gear, and lights the way.

The Ghost. The Ghost is a Tiny Construct with AC 14, Hit Points equal to 5 times your Proficiency Bonus, and a Fly Speed of 40 feet (hover). It acts on your turn, can't make attacks, and talks; the DM decides its personality.

Light. At will, the Ghost sheds Bright Light in a 20-foot radius and Dim Light for an additional 20 feet, or dims itself to a soft glow.

Scan. As a Magic action, the Ghost scans a creature or object within 30 feet of it. For a creature, you learn its damage Vulnerabilities, Resistances, and Immunities, and whether it has fewer than half its Hit Points remaining. For an object, you learn its magical properties as if you had cast Identify on it. The Ghost can do this a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and it regains all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Translate. The Ghost understands every spoken and written language it hears or sees, and it can relay the meaning to you telepathically.

Hack. Once per Long Rest, the Ghost can open one lock or mechanism you can see within 30 feet, as if casting Knock, but silently. While the Ghost is within 5 feet of you, you have Advantage on ability checks to operate, disarm, or repair a mechanism.

Transmat. The Ghost carries your gear in a pocket of Light: up to 50 pounds of nonliving objects. As a Bonus Action, it can deliver a stored item to your hand or take one from you.

Waypoint. Once per Long Rest, you can mark your current location. While you're on the same plane of existence, you always know the direction and distance to it.

Telepathic Link. You and the Ghost can communicate telepathically while within 1 mile of each other, and as a Bonus Action you can see through its eyes until the start of your next turn.

Into the Shell. As a Bonus Action, you can tuck the Ghost away into its shell, where it can't be targeted, or call it out to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. If the Ghost drops to 0 Hit Points, its shell goes dark, and it reawakens at the next dawn.

Optional Rule: Revive. For Destiny-setting games where the players are Risen, the DM can add this property: once per Long Rest, when you die, the Ghost restores you to life with 1 Hit Point after 1 minute, unless the Ghost is destroyed first. This is a campaign decision, not part of the base item.

Rare · Exotic armor

Cyrtarachne's Facade

Wondrous Item (Helm), Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic Armor

Spun silk, worn as armor. A Strand Hunter piece in Destiny, where grappling wove a protective mesh around its wearer. It uses the Woven Mail keyword from the Destiny Overview & Chassis. While wearing this helm, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

Silk Grapple. After you move at least 15 feet on your turn by any means other than walking (such as jumping, climbing, teleporting, or grappling), you gain Woven Mail until the start of your next turn.

Threadrunner Hook. If you have the Threadrunner's Grapple, each use of it grants you Woven Mail until the start of your next turn.

Rare · Exotic

Quicksilver Storm

Weapon (Net), Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic

Woven from living thread. It doesn't just catch you; it lifts you. In Destiny, this Strand auto rifle carried a built-in grenade mode. As a net, it's the most Strand weapon imaginable: it tangles, suspends, and severs. It uses the Suspended and Severed keywords from the Destiny Overview & Chassis. You gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls made with this magic weapon.

Living Thread. When you hit a Large or smaller creature with the net, it is also Suspended until the start of your next turn, in addition to the net's normal effect. The net then whips back to your hand.

Grenade Mode. As an action, you hurl the net open at a point you can see within 30 feet. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus plus your Dexterity modifier) or be Severed and have its Speed reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn. Once you use this property, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

Rare · Exotic · Ahamkara

Wish-Keeper

Weapon (Longbow), Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic, Ahamkara

Strung with a wish. Every arrow binds. In Destiny, this Strand bow from the Season of the Wish fires snare arrows that suspend their targets. It uses the Suspended and Severed keywords from the Destiny Overview & Chassis, and it shares the Ahamkara rules (Whispers, Boon, and Debts; see Ahamkara Items on the Destiny Exotics Overview). You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Snare Arrow. When you hit a creature with this bow, you can make it Suspended and Severed. You can use this property a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Very Rare · Exotic

Final Warning

Weapon (Throwing Knives, set of three), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic

Everyone gets one warning. In Destiny, this Strand sidearm marks its targets and then unravels them. Here it's a set of three throwing knives (each a dagger: Finesse, Light, Thrown) that return to your hand after a thrown attack; a single attunement covers the set. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with these magic weapons. They use the Unraveled keyword from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

Mark of Warning. Each time you hit a creature with one of the knives, it gains a mark (maximum 3 marks per creature). As a Bonus Action, you can call in the marks on one creature you can see: it takes 1d8 Psychic damage per mark, becomes Unraveled, and loses all its marks.

Uncommon · Exotic

Hawkmoon

Weapon (Handaxe), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

Feeling lucky? In Destiny, this hand cannon's rounds were a gamble: any one of them might hit far harder than the rest. As a light, throwable handaxe, it keeps that "maybe this one" feeling.

Luck in the Chamber. At dawn each day, secretly roll a d6 and note the number. The first time on each of your turns that you roll that number on the axe's damage die, the hit deals an extra 2d6 Force damage.

Forged Swords

Campaign hook: in D1 the three forged swords (Raze-Lighter, Bolt-Caster, Dark-Drinker) began as plain "Edge" swords reforged through a quest, so a DM can award the Uncommon version early and let the party reforge it into a stronger Rare version later.

Uncommon · Exotic

Raze-Lighter

Weapon (Greatsword), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

"Now take it in hand and feed its flames."

Phoenix Uppercut. Once per dawn, when you hit a creature with this sword, you can deal an extra 2d6 Fire damage. If the target is Large or smaller, it's launched 10 feet into the air, then falls, taking 1d6 Bludgeoning damage and landing with the Prone condition.

Uncommon · Exotic

Bolt-Caster

Weapon (Longsword), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

"Now take it in hand and unleash its thunder."

Sword of Thunder. Once per dawn, as a Magic action, you swing the sword and fire a bolt of lightning in a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Each creature in the line makes a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. A thundercloud then lingers along the line until the start of your next turn; a creature that enters it takes 1d6 Lightning damage.

Uncommon · Exotic

Dark-Drinker

Weapon (Greatsword), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

"Now take it in hand and slake its thirst."

Supermassive Vortex. Once per dawn, as a Magic action, you spin in a whirl of Void. Each creature within 5 feet of you makes a DC 13 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 2d8 Force damage and is pushed 10 feet away from you. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.
