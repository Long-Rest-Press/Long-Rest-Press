Iron Lord Arms

Weapons that carry the names of the Iron Lords. They make fitting rewards for high Renown with the order.

Very Rare · Non-Exotic · Iron Lord Arms

Jolder's Hammer

Weapon (Warhammer), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Non-Exotic, Iron Lord Arms

The champion's hammer, carried by the Lord who sealed the door. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Champion's Challenge. As a Bonus Action, you challenge a creature you can see within 30 feet for 1 minute. While challenged, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you, and your hits against it with the hammer deal an extra 1d8 Lightning damage.

Seal the Door. Once per Long Rest, when you drop to 0 Hit Points, you drop to 1 Hit Point instead, and each ally within 30 feet of you gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your level.

Rare · Non-Exotic · Iron Lord Arms

Efrideet's Spear

Weapon (Spear), Rare (Requires Attunement), Non-Exotic, Iron Lord Arms

The order's sharpshooter fought hardest when angry. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. Its thrown range increases to 40/120 feet, and it flies back to your hand immediately after a thrown attack.

Righteous Anger. When an ally you can see drops to 0 Hit Points, or when you become Bloodied, your attacks with the spear deal an extra 1d8 Piercing damage until the end of your next turn.

Rare · Non-Exotic · Iron Lord Arms

Timur's Lash

Weapon (Whip), Rare (Requires Attunement), Non-Exotic, Iron Lord Arms

A storm on a string, rebuilt a dozen times by a man who couldn't stop improving it. Lord Timur was a Stormcaller, a fearless warrior, and a researcher who loved inventing new ways to fight. In Destiny, Timur's Lash is a hand cannon named after a weapon he once owned; here it's a lightning whip. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, and its hits deal an extra 1d6 Lightning damage.

Tinkerer's Adjustments. When you finish a Long Rest, choose one of the following settings. It lasts until you choose again.

  • Chain. Once per turn, when you hit with the whip, lightning arcs to a second creature within 10 feet of the target, dealing 1d6 Lightning damage.

  • Snare. Once per turn, when you hit a Large or smaller creature with the whip, you can pull it up to 10 feet toward you.

  • Shock. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with the whip, it can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn.

Very Rare · Non-Exotic · Iron Lord Arms

Saladin's Iron Battle Axe

Weapon (Greataxe), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Non-Exotic, Iron Lord Arms

He trusted only Radegast. He outlived them all. When Saladin and Efrideet faced an Ahamkara, Efrideet hung back with her rifle while Saladin closed in with his Iron Battle Axe to fight the dragon like a knight of old. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Knight of Old. Your hits with the axe deal an extra 1d10 Slashing damage to Large or larger creatures, and you have Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Frightened condition caused by such creatures.

Deaf to Wishes. While you're attuned to the axe, you can't be Charmed by Dragons, and you can't accept an Ahamkara Boon. Your hits with the axe deal an extra 2d8 damage to Dragons and to creatures attuned to an Ahamkara item.

Last of the Iron Lords. While you're Bloodied, you can take a Bonus Action to stand defiant for 1 minute. For the duration, you have Resistance to all damage, and your hits with the axe deal an extra 1d8 Fire damage. Once you use this property, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

Uncommon · Non-Exotic

Iron Lords' Memories

Trinket Set (Eight Pendants), Uncommon (Each Piece; No Attunement), Non-Exotic

Iron pendants, each holding something of a fallen Lord. Wear them, and the order fights through you. In Destiny's Rise of Iron, the archivist Tyra Karn gave out eight artifacts, each a "Memory of" a Lord who died fighting SIVA, with an ability that matched the Lord's personality. In a fantasy game, Tyra becomes the archivist of the Iron Temple, who awards Memories for Renown, great deeds, or victory in the Iron Banner.

Wearing Memories. A creature can wear up to three Memories at once. If it wears more, the dead crowd in, and none of them function.

MemoryBenefit
Memory of RadegastWhen a ranged attack hits you while you're wielding a melee weapon, you can take a Reaction to reduce the damage by 1d10 plus your Proficiency Bonus. If this reduces the damage to 0, you can knock the missile back at a creature within 30 feet, using the triggering attack's damage.
Memory of PerunYou always know which creatures within 60 feet of you are Bloodied or Concentrating on a spell.
Memory of JolderWhen you take the Dash action, your Speed increases by 10 feet for that turn, and you never gain Exhaustion from a forced march.
Memory of SilimarYou have Resistance to damage dealt by an ongoing effect at the start or end of your turn (such as Burning or a lingering poison), and you have Advantage on saving throws to end ongoing effects.
Memory of GheleonYou have Tremorsense with a range of 30 feet.
Memory of SkorriWhen you roll Initiative, each ally within 30 feet of you gains Temporary Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
Memory of TimurOnce per Long Rest, when you hit a creature of CR 1 or lower with a melee attack, it must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or have the Charmed condition for 1 minute, fighting on your side. The effect ends early if you or your allies deal damage to it.
Memory of FelwinterYou can't be Charmed, and magic can't read your thoughts: Felwinter's Lie, keeping his secret.

Destiny Subclass Hooks.

  • Memory of Felwinter. If you have a Super, you can choose to give it up while wearing this Memory. If you do, you regain 1 Grenade use at the start of each combat, and you can use your Charged Melee twice per turn.

  • Memory of Skorri. When you use your Super, one ally within 30 feet of you regains one expended Grenade use.

Set Bonuses

  • Two Memories: Iron Oath. You have Advantage on Death Saving Throws.

  • Three Memories: Iron Lord. Once per Long Rest, when an ally within 30 feet of you drops to 0 Hit Points, they drop to 1 Hit Point instead.

Very Rare · Exotic

The Young Wolf's Howl

Weapon (Greatsword), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic

"To the first of the new Iron Lords." You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Tempered Light. While you wield this sword, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

Howling Flames. The sword has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a Magic action, you can expend 1 charge to slam the blade down, sending fire racing along the ground in a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Each creature in the line makes a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d8 Fire damage and is Burning, and until the end of your next turn, attacks against it deal an extra 1d4 Fire damage. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

Thrive by the Sword. Once per turn, when you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with this sword, it regains 1 expended charge.

Legendary · Exotic

Gjallarhorn

Wondrous Item (Horn), Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster), Exotic

A war horn forged from the armor of fallen Guardians. When it sounds, the wolves answer.

Spellcasting Focus. A Bard, or any spellcaster who can use a musical instrument as a focus, can use the horn as a spellcasting focus. While holding it, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and to your spell save DC.

Wolfpack Rounds. The horn has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. As a Magic action, you can expend 1 charge to sound the horn and launch a screaming missile of fire at a point within 150 feet. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw against DC 18 or your spell save DC, whichever is higher, taking 8d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. Four Wolfpack Rounds then split off and seek creatures of your choice within 30 feet of that point; each deals 2d6 Fire damage with no attack roll or saving throw, and you can stack them on one target.

Pack Hunter. When you use Wolfpack Rounds, each ally within 30 feet of you who can hear the horn gains a wolfpack round of their own. The first time each of them hits with an attack before the end of your next turn, that attack deals an extra 2d6 Fire damage.
