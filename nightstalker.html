Nightstalker

Nightstalkers hunt from the edge of the dark, pinning prey with threads of Void so their allies can finish the job.

v0.1 PlaytestDestiny Hunter · VoidInspired by Destiny 2
Download PDF
Shared Destiny rules →This subclass uses the Grenade, Charged Melee, Class Ability, Super, and element keyword rules from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

The Nightstalker is the Void Hunter as a Ranger subclass, built as a support Hunter around tethering enemies so the whole party hits harder. It uses the Shared Chassis from the Destiny Overview & Chassis, with its Super at level 11 and the upgrade at level 15. Void uses Force damage, and the key ability is Wisdom.

Level 3

Shadow Tether

When you cast Hunter's Mark, you can make it a Shadow Tether instead of its normal effect. Choose the target and up to two other creatures within 15 feet of it. Each becomes Tethered for the spell's duration. A Tethered creature has these effects:

  • Its Speed is reduced by 10 feet.
  • The first time each turn it's hit by an attack from you or an ally, it takes an extra 1d4 Force damage. This increases to 1d6 at Ranger level 11.

Whenever you could move Hunter's Mark to a new creature, you can instead tether a new group in the same way.

Level 3

Suppressor Grenade

This is your Grenade. As a Bonus Action, you throw a grenade at a point you can see within 60 feet. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point makes a Constitution saving throw.

  • Failed save: The creature takes your grenade damage as Force damage and is Suppressed until the start of your next turn. A Suppressed creature can't take Reactions and has Disadvantage on saving throws to maintain Concentration.
  • Successful save: The creature takes half as much damage and isn't Suppressed.
Level 3

Snare Bomb

This is your Charged Melee. Once per turn, when you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a thrown Snare Bomb aimed at a point within 30 feet. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of that point makes a Dexterity saving throw.

  • Failed save: The creature takes Force damage equal to your grenade damage, and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
  • Successful save: The creature takes half as much damage, and its Speed isn't reduced.
Level 7

Vanish in Smoke

When you throw a Snare Bomb, you can make yourself and up to two allies within 10 feet of the point Invisible until the start of your next turn. The invisibility ends early for a creature if it makes an attack roll, deals damage, or casts a spell.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Level 7

Dodge

You gain the Dodge Class Ability from the Shared Chassis. Nightstalker twist (Vanishing Step): when you Dodge, you also have the Invisible condition until the start of your next turn. The invisibility ends early if you make an attack roll, deal damage, or cast a spell.

Level 11

Super: Shadowshot

When you finish a Long Rest, choose Deadfall or Moebius Quiver as your Super. You can regain its use by expending a level 3+ spell slot (no action required).

Deadfall

As an action, you fire a Void arrow at a point you can see within 120 feet. It plants an anchor that lasts for 3 rounds, ending at the start of your turn 3 rounds later.

When the anchor lands, each creature of your choice within 20 feet of it makes a Dexterity saving throw. A creature also makes the save when it enters that area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there. On a failed save, the creature becomes Deadfall-Tethered until the anchor fades. A Deadfall-Tethered creature has these effects:

  • It's Tethered (see Shadow Tether), its Speed is halved, and it can't take Reactions.
  • Shared Fate. Once per turn, when a Deadfall-Tethered creature takes damage from an attack, each other Deadfall-Tethered creature takes Force damage equal to half that damage.

Moebius Quiver

As an action, you fire three tether arrows, each at a creature you can see within 120 feet. You can target the same creature more than once. Make a ranged attack roll for each arrow. On a hit, the target takes 4d10 Force damage and becomes Tethered for 1 minute. An arrow that hits a creature that's already Tethered deals an extra 2d10 Force damage.

Level 15

Heart of the Hunt

Both Supers. You no longer choose a Super when you finish a Long Rest. Each time you use Shadowshot, choose Deadfall or Moebius Quiver.

Spreading Tether. When a Tethered creature drops to 0 Hit Points, you can Tether another creature within 15 feet of it (no action required). The new Tether lasts as long as the original would have.
