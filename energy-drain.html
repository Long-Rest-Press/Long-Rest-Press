Energy Drain

The Voidwalker's life-draining melee cantrip: strike with the Void and turn the damage into Temporary Hit Points.

v0.1 PlaytestVoidwalkerInspired by Destiny 2
From the Voidwalker →Voidwalker wizards learn this cantrip at level 3 as their Charged Melee. It doesn't count against the number of cantrips they know.

Energy Drain

Evocation Cantrip (Voidwalker)

Casting TimeAction
RangeTouch
ComponentsV, S
DurationInstantaneous

Make a melee spell attack against a creature. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Force damage, and you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the damage dealt.

Cantrip Upgrade. The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).

Playtest note
  • Temporary Hit Points. They're gained every turn, though they don't stack. If that runs hot, grant half the damage dealt instead.
