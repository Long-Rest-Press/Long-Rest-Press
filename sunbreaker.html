Sunbreaker

Sunbreakers are forge-priests of the Light. They hammer the battlefield into holy ground and fight from the middle of the fire.

v0.1 PlaytestDestiny Titan · SolarInspired by Destiny 2
Download PDF
Shared Destiny rules →This subclass uses the Grenade, Charged Melee, Class Ability, Super, and element keyword rules from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

The Sunbreaker is the Solar Titan as a Cleric domain. Cleric subclasses normally gain features only at levels 3, 6, and 17, so the Sunbreaker adds a level 10 feature for its Super. Solar uses Fire damage, or Radiant for holy effects. The key ability is Wisdom, and the Protector Divine Order is the natural fit for its heavy armor and martial weapons.

Level 3

Sunspots (Sol Invictus)

Several of your features create Sunspots. A Sunspot is a 5-foot-radius circle of sunfire on the ground that lasts until the end of your next turn. You can have up to three at once; if you create a fourth, your oldest one fades.

  • You and your allies: A creature of your choice that starts its turn in a Sunspot regains Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). A creature can regain Hit Points from your Sunspots only once per round.
  • Enemies: A creature that enters a Sunspot or starts its turn there takes 1d6 Fire damage.

Once per turn, when you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with Fire or Radiant damage, you can create a Sunspot in its space.

Level 3

Channel Divinity: Consecration

As a Magic action, you expend one use of your Channel Divinity to slam the ground and send out a ring of solar fire. Each creature of your choice in a 15-foot Emanation originating from you makes a Dexterity saving throw.

  • Failed save: The creature takes Fire damage equal to 2d10 plus your Cleric level, and if it's Large or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
  • Successful save: The creature takes half as much damage only.

You then create a Sunspot in your space. It doesn't count against your limit of three.

Level 3

Thermite Grenade

This is your Grenade. As a Bonus Action, you hurl a grenade that skids along the ground in a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Each creature in the line makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking your grenade damage as Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. You can create a Sunspot at the end of the line.

Level 3

Throwing Hammer

This is your Charged Melee. You learn the Throwing Hammer cantrip, which doesn't count against the number of cantrips you know.

Throwing Hammer

Evocation Cantrip (Sunbreaker)

  • Casting Time: Action
  • Range: 30 feet
  • Components: V, S
  • Duration: Instantaneous

Make a ranged spell attack against a creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Fire damage.

The hammer then lands in an unoccupied space next to the target and stays there until the end of your next turn. If you move into its space before then, you pick it up (no action required) and gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier plus your Proficiency Bonus. If the hammer reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, it creates a Sunspot where it lands.

Cantrip Upgrade. The damage increases by 1d10 when you reach levels 5 (2d10), 11 (3d10), and 17 (4d10).

Level 6

Barricade

You gain the Barricade Class Ability from the Shared Chassis. When you gain this feature, choose one of the following twists.

Khepri's Horn. When you raise a Barricade, a wave of solar fire rolls out from it in a 30-foot-long, 10-foot-wide line. Each creature in the line makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking your grenade damage as Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one.

Sunforge Barricade. Your Barricade creates a Sunspot at each end. These Sunspots last as long as the Barricade does and don't count against your limit of three.

Level 10

Super

When you finish a Long Rest, choose Hammer of Sol or Burning Maul as your Super. You can regain its use by expending a level 5+ spell slot (no action required). Either Super lasts for 3 rounds, ending at the start of your turn 3 rounds later. While it lasts, you have Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage, and your Speed increases by 10 feet.

Hammer of Sol

As a Bonus Action, you forge a blazing hammer. While it lasts, you can take a Magic action to throw two hammers at creatures within 120 feet, making a ranged spell attack for each. On a hit, a hammer deals 3d10 Fire damage, and each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 2d6 Fire damage. Each hammer creates a Sunspot where it lands; these don't count against your limit of three.

Burning Maul

As a Bonus Action, you take up a burning maul. While it lasts, you can take a Magic action to make one of these strikes:

  • Spin. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one.
  • Slam. Each creature in a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 5d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. You leave Sunspots at the line's midpoint and end.
Level 17

Sun Warrior

Both Supers. You no longer choose a Super when you finish a Long Rest. Each time you use your Super, choose Hammer of Sol or Burning Maul.

Radiant. You and your allies deal an extra 1d6 Radiant damage the first time each turn you hit with an attack while standing in one of your Sunspots.

Eternal Sun. Your Sunspots last for 1 minute, and you can have up to six at once.
