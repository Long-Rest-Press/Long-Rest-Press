Striker

Strikers are the storm given fists. They close the distance, hit like a thunderclap, and get faster with every enemy that drops.

v0.1 PlaytestTitan · ArcInspired by Destiny 2
Shared Destiny rules →This subclass uses the Grenade, Charged Melee, Class Ability, Super, and element keyword rules from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

The Striker is the Arc Titan as a Barbarian subclass. Lightning is the Arc damage type. The key ability is Strength, so the save DC is 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Strength modifier.

Level 3

Storm Fists

Your Unarmed Strikes deal 1d8 damage plus your Strength modifier. Each time you hit, you choose whether the damage is Bludgeoning or Lightning. Your Unarmed Strikes also have the Push weapon mastery property.

In the 2024 rules, Rage damage and Brutal Strike already work with Strength-based Unarmed Strikes. Weapons are rewarded but not required.

Level 3

Flashbang

This is your Grenade. As a Bonus Action, you throw a crackling charge at a point you can see within 60 feet. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point makes a Constitution saving throw.

  • Failed save: The creature takes your grenade damage as Lightning damage and has the Blinded condition until the start of your next turn.
  • Successful save: The creature takes half as much damage and isn't Blinded.

You can throw a Flashbang while Raging.

Level 3

Shoulder Charge

This is your Charged Melee. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike after moving at least 20 feet in a straight line toward it this turn, the target takes extra Lightning damage equal to your grenade damage. If the target is Large or smaller, it must also succeed on a Strength saving throw or have the Prone condition.

Level 6

Amplified

While Raging, when you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points or score a Critical Hit with a melee attack, you become Amplified until your Rage ends. While Amplified, you gain these benefits:

  • Your Speed increases by 10 feet.
  • Shoulder Charge requires only 10 feet of movement.
  • Opportunity Attacks against you have Disadvantage.
Level 6

Barricade

You gain the Barricade Class Ability from the Shared Chassis. Striker twist: when you raise a Barricade while Raging, you become Amplified.

Level 10

Super

When you finish a Long Rest, choose Fists of Havoc or Thundercrash as your Super. You can regain its use by expending two uses of your Rage (no action required).

Fists of Havoc

As a Bonus Action, you become a living storm for 3 rounds. The effect ends at the start of your turn 3 rounds later. If you aren't Raging, you enter your Rage without expending a use. While Fists of Havoc lasts, you gain these benefits:

  • Storm Body. Your Speed increases by 10 feet, and your Unarmed Strikes deal an extra 1d8 Lightning damage.
  • Unstoppable. Shoulder Charge has no movement requirement.
  • Seismic Slam. When you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a slam. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 4d8 Lightning damage and has the Prone condition. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

Thundercrash

As an action, you launch yourself up to 60 feet in a straight line, flying without provoking Opportunity Attacks, and land in an unoccupied space. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of where you land makes a Dexterity saving throw.

  • Failed save: The creature takes 10d10 Lightning damage, and if it's Large or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
  • Successful save: The creature takes half as much damage only.
Level 14

Lightning Unbound

Both Supers. You no longer choose a Super when you finish a Long Rest. Each time you use your Super, choose Fists of Havoc or Thundercrash.

Jolt. When a creature takes damage from your Flashbang, Shoulder Charge, or Super, it becomes Jolted until the end of your next turn. Jolted and Amplified are defined under Element Keywords in the Shared Chassis. Jolt is the Arc counterpart to the Voidwalker's Volatile.
