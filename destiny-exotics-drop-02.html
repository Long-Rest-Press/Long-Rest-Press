Very Rare · Exotic Armor

Phoenix Protocol

Wondrous Item (Chest Piece), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic Armor

Light feeds Light. Every life saved stokes the fire. A Warlock piece in Destiny. While wearing it, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

Kindled Healing. When you restore Hit Points to a creature with a spell or feature, one other ally of your choice within 10 feet of that creature regains Hit Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

Dawnblade Hook. If you have the Dawnblade's Well of Radiance Super, your Well lasts 1 extra round. Once per Long Rest, if an ally inside your Well reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, you regain your use of your Super when the Well ends.

Uncommon · Exotic Armor

Transversive Steps

Wondrous Item (Boots), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic Armor

Always moving, always ready. A Warlock piece in Destiny.

Fleet Step. Your Speed increases by 10 feet. When you take the Dash action, you can draw or stow one weapon or spellcasting focus as part of it, and your next ranged attack before the end of your turn ignores the Loading property.

Very Rare · Exotic

Dragon's Breath

Wand (Spellcasting Focus), Very Rare (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster), Exotic

A trail of fire that waits, then bites. In Destiny, this Solar rocket launcher leaves a burning trail that erupts a moment later. It uses the Burning and Ignite keywords from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

Spellcasting Focus. You can use this wand as a spellcasting focus. While holding it, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls and to your spell save DC.

Dragon's Breath. The wand has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a Magic action, you can expend 1 charge to fire a blazing trail in a 60-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Each creature in the line makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC, becoming Burning on a failed save. At the start of your next turn, each creature still Burning from this trail Ignites.

Uncommon · Exotic

Edge of Concurrence

Weapon (Trident), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

Three prongs, three targets. In Destiny, the Hunter's Exotic glaive fired seeking chain lightning. As a trident, its prongs split the strike. It uses the Jolted keyword from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

Chain Lightning. When you hit a creature with the trident, lightning can leap from it to up to two other creatures within 15 feet of it. Each takes 1d8 Lightning damage and is Jolted. Once you use this property, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

Uncommon · Exotic

Jötunn

Weapon (Greatclub), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

A giant's club, still warm from the forge of the sky. The name comes from the jötnar, the giants of Norse myth. In Destiny, this Solar fusion rifle lobbed a slow, homing fireball instead of a burst. It uses the Burning keyword from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

Charge Shot. When you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a swing that launches a homing fireball at a creature you can see within 60 feet. It curves around obstacles, so the target gains no benefit from Half Cover or Three-Quarters Cover. The target makes a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus plus your Strength modifier), taking 2d8 Fire damage and becoming Burning on a failed save, or taking half as much damage on a successful one. Once you use this property, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

Uncommon · Exotic

Sturm & Drang

Weapon (Longsword and Dagger, pair), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

Storm and stress. In Destiny, these paired pistols fed each other: kills with one reloaded and empowered the other. As blades, they reward fighting with both at once. Sturm is a longsword and Drang is a dagger; a single attunement covers both.

Storm and Stress. When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with Drang, Sturm is overcharged: your next hit with Sturm before the end of your next turn deals an extra 2d6 damage. When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with Sturm, your next hit with Drang before the end of your next turn deals an extra 1d6 damage.

Legendary · Exotic

Ticcu's Divination

Weapon (Longbow), Legendary (Requires Attunement), Exotic

You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, and its arrows deal an extra 1d6 Fire damage on a hit.

Celestial Eye. The bow's arrows home in on their targets. They ignore Half Cover and Three-Quarters Cover, and you don't have Disadvantage on attack rolls at long range.

Cataclysm. The bow has 3 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. As a Magic action, you can expend 1 charge to loose a blazing arrow at a point within 150 feet. It bursts into a cluster of seeking bolts: each creature of your choice within 20 feet of that point makes a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d10 Fire damage and becoming Burning on a failed save, or taking half as much damage on a successful one.

Legendary · Exotic

One Thousand Voices

Wondrous Item (Spellcasting Focus), Legendary (Requires Attunement by a Bard), Exotic

Sing, and a thousand voices sing with you. Not all of them are kind. In Destiny, this fusion rifle is made from the eye of Riven, an Ahamkara (a wish-dragon whose voice is a thousand voices at once). Its beam carves a line through enemies, then the line erupts in a chain of delayed explosions.

Spellcasting Focus. You can use this item as a spellcasting focus for your Bard spells. While holding it, you gain a +3 bonus to spell attack rolls and to your spell save DC.

The Choir. Once per turn, when you deal damage with a Bard spell that has a Verbal component, the choir joins in and one target of the spell takes an extra 1d8 Psychic damage.

Ahamkara's Eye. The focus has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. As a Magic action, you can expend 1 charge to sing one impossible note that becomes a beam of fire in a 120-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Each creature in the line makes a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 6d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. At the start of your next turn, the whole line erupts in a chain of explosions: each creature within 10 feet of the line makes another Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one.

Whispers of the Ahamkara. The voices offer secrets, shortcuts, and help. One Thousand Voices uses the Ahamkara rules for Whispers, Boons, and Debts (see Ahamkara Items on the Destiny Exotics Overview).
