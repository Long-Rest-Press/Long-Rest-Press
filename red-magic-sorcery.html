Red Magic Sorcery

Red Mages blend black and white magic with the rapier, fencing in close and leaping back out to cast.

v0.1 PlaytestThe Red MageInspired by Final Fantasy
Download PDF

Inspired by the Red Mage of Final Fantasy Tactics Advance 2, with touches of Final Fantasy XIV. Their signature is doubling up: twinning spells early, stacking Metamagic later, and finally casting two spells at once.

Level 3

Red Mage Training

You gain proficiency with Light armor and with Martial weapons that have the Finesse property. You can use such a weapon as a Spellcasting Focus for your Sorcerer spells.

Level 3

Red Magic Spells

When you reach a Sorcerer level specified in the table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

Level 3

Dualcast

When you use your Twinned Spell Metamagic, you can make it cost 0 Sorcery Points. You can do so a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Level 6

Extra Attack

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. You can replace one of those attacks with a casting of one of your Sorcerer cantrips that has a casting time of an action.

Level 6

Displacement

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks. This movement doesn't count against your Speed.

Level 14

Chainspell

Your Twinned Spell Metamagic doesn't count against the limit on Metamagic options you can apply to a single spell. You can apply Twinned Spell and one other Metamagic option to the same spell.

Level 18

Doublecast

Once per turn, when you cast a spell using a spell slot, you can spend 5 Sorcery Points to cast a second spell of level 5 or lower as part of the same action. This second spell can expend a spell slot even though you've already expended one this turn. Its casting time must be an action or a Bonus Action.

Playtest notes
  • Twinned in 2024: Twinned Spell only works on spells that gain targets when upcast. Check that Dualcast has enough good targets at low levels to feel impactful.
  • Displacement: free 10 feet every turn after a melee hit. Watch whether the Red Mage becomes impossible for melee enemies to pin down.
  • Survivability: d6 Hit Dice and Light armor in melee. See whether Displacement is enough defense, or whether Medium armor is needed after all.
  • Doublecast cap: started at level 5 or lower for the second spell and 5 Sorcery Points per use. Adjust after playtesting.
  • Spell list overlap: confirm none of the Red Magic Spells are already on the 2024 Sorcerer list; swap in a replacement if so.
