The Iron Lords

A faction of knights for the Dark Age: people who came back from death with strange power, and who chose to protect the powerless instead of ruling them.

Who They Are

In the Dark Age, the gift of the Risen fell on ordinary people with no rules and no masters. Many used it to rule. Radegast, a Risen knight, grew sick of watching the gifted prey on the ungifted, and gathered a few he trusted to fight back in secret. They became the Iron Lords: an order of knights who protect settlements, break Warlords, and hold each other to a code. A Warlord once sneered at them as "Iron Wolves"; the name stuck, and became the name of their most famous company.

The Code of Iron

The order's tenets (a homebrew summary of their ideals):

  • Shield the Ungifted. The gift exists to protect those without it.
  • Never Rule. An Iron Lord may defend a village but never claim it.
  • Answer to Each Other. No Lord is above the order's judgment.
  • Settle It in the Open. Disputes between Lords are decided by trial of arms, never by knives in the dark.

Goals

  • Break the Warlords one by one and free their villages.
  • Build safe settlements that can stand without Risen protection.
  • Later: recover the living iron (SIVA) to rebuild the world, the goal that will destroy them.

Allies and Enemies

  • Allies: freed villages, wandering Risen who share their ideals, and (secretly) Felwinter's hidden knowledge.
  • Enemies: the Warlords, raider clans, and, in the end, the war-mind under the mountain.

Ranks and Renown

Characters earn Renown with the Iron Lords by protecting settlements, breaking Warlords, and honoring the Code of Iron. Breaking the Code costs Renown, and a serious breach brings a trial of arms.

1SquireFree lodging and meals at the mountain fortress and any settlement the order protects

The Iron Banner

The order's tournament of arms, held at the mountain fortress. Risen from across the land compete in duels, team melees, and contests of skill, and the order watches for recruits. Use it as a set-piece adventure: rivalries, sabotage by a Warlord's agents, and a chance for the party to win Renown, gear, and a Lord's attention in a single session.

Adventure Hooks

  1. The Champion's Duel. A Warlord agrees to settle a village's fate by single combat. Jolder wants a party member as her second.
  2. Silimar's Tower. Raiders have knocked down Silimar's tower again. He asks the party to help hold it while he rebuilds, and he won't say why it matters.
  3. The Wolf's Riddle. Felwinter sends the party into fallen-empire ruins with oddly specific instructions, and they find doors that open for him alone.
  4. The Second-in-Command. Perun's plan to topple a Warlord needs someone on the inside. The party goes undercover in the tyrant's court.
  5. The Vault Beneath the Mountain. Rumors spread of living iron that could rebuild the world. The order begins to dream of it.

The Long Shadow

The Dark Age era ends with SIVA: the order marches into the war-mind's vault to claim the living iron, and almost none of them come back. In a Dark Age campaign, this is the endgame looming over every victory.
