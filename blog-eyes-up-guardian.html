The Next Two Weeks: Eyes Up, Guardian

The Light is back. For the next two weeks, every release on Long Rest Press comes from Destiny.

If you've been following along, you've already met the Voidwalker, the Striker, and the Sunslinger, and you may have read the Destiny overview that lays out how all of these subclasses work. If you haven't, start there. Every Destiny subclass runs on the same chassis: a grenade, a charged melee, a class ability like a Rift or a Barricade, and a Super that turns your character into a walking boss fight for a round or two.

None of these are new classes, and none of them need guns. Each one is a subclass for a class you already know, so they drop straight into any sword-and-sorcery game.

What's coming

  • Mon, Nov 16 — Dawnblade (Warlock). Wings of flame and a sword of the sun. Hang in the air raining fire on the battlefield, or plant a Well of Radiance and keep your whole party standing.
  • Thu, Nov 19 — Nightstalker (Ranger). The rare support Hunter. Tether your enemies together with Void so every hit your party lands spreads across the whole pack.
  • Mon, Nov 23 — Sunbreaker (Cleric). Hammers of the sun, burning ground, and Barricades that answer with a wall of fire. Divine wrath, Titan style.
  • Thu, Nov 26 — Sentinel (Paladin). Shield up. Throw it, bash with it, or raise a Ward of Dawn and turn a corner of the battlefield into a place your enemies can't touch.

That gives you a caster, a skirmisher, and two frontliners: one healer-hammer and one wall.

Bring loot

I'm also releasing the Destiny Exotics a few at a time with each subclass, so keep an eye on the bottom of every post. Not every one is a gun turned into a crossbow, I promise. Maybe a couple of them are, I just couldn't resist.

A heads-up

These subclasses shine brightest in games with lots of minions. If your table loves fighting hordes, Guardians will feel right at home.

And yes, there are more Guardians coming. Stay tuned for those down the road.

See you in the Tower.
