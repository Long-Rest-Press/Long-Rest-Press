Dawnblade

Dawnblades are warriors of the sunrise. They fight from the sky with flame in their hands and a burning sword when it counts.

v0.1 PlaytestDestiny Warlock · SolarInspired by Destiny 2
Download PDF
Shared Destiny rules →This subclass uses the Grenade, Charged Melee, Class Ability, Super, and element keyword rules from the Destiny Overview & Chassis, including the Burning and Ignite keywords.

The Dawnblade is the Solar Destiny warlock as a Warlock subclass. Pact of the Blade is the thematic best fit, but it's never required: every feature works with spells, and Blade pact only adds options. Solar uses Fire damage, or Radiant for healing and holy effects. The key ability is Charisma.

Level 3

Incendiary Grenade

This is your Grenade. As a Bonus Action, you throw a grenade at a point you can see within 60 feet. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius sphere centered on that point makes a Dexterity saving throw.

  • Failed save: The creature takes your grenade damage as Fire damage and is Burning. Burning is defined under Element Keywords in the Shared Chassis.
  • Successful save: The creature takes half as much damage and doesn't catch fire.
Level 3

Celestial Fire

This is your Charged Melee. You learn the Celestial Fire cantrip, which doesn't count against the number of cantrips you know.

Celestial Fire

Evocation Cantrip (Dawnblade)

  • Casting Time: Action
  • Range: 60 feet
  • Components: V, S
  • Duration: Instantaneous

You hurl a spiral of three small flames at a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 Fire damage.

Cantrip Upgrade. The damage increases by 1d10 when you reach levels 5 (2d10), 11 (3d10), and 17 (4d10).

Blade Synergy. If you have Pact of the Blade, you can cast this cantrip in place of one of the attacks when you take the Attack action.

Level 3

Wings of Sacred Dawn

When you're in the air and make a ranged spell attack on your turn, you can choose to hover instead of falling. You stay at your current height until the end of your next turn. You can keep this up by making a ranged spell attack on each of your turns, for up to 2 rounds in total.

After that, or if you skip an attack, you descend 60 feet per round and take no falling damage. You can't hover this way again until you've touched the ground.

This works with Eldritch Blast, Celestial Fire, or any other ranged spell attack. The Otherworldly Leap invocation gets you airborne at will.

Level 6

Rift and Heat Rises

Rift. You gain the Rift Class Ability from the Shared Chassis. Dawnblade twist (Sky Rift): you can create your Rift while airborne. It forms on the ground beneath you, and you count as inside it while you're within 30 feet directly above it.

Heat Rises. As a Bonus Action, you can expend one use of your Grenade to launch yourself up to 30 feet straight up without provoking Opportunity Attacks. For the next minute, Wings of Sacred Dawn has no 2-round limit.

Level 10

Super

When you finish a Long Rest, choose Daybreak or Well of Radiance as your Super. You can regain its use by expending a Pact Magic spell slot (no action required). Either Super lasts for 3 rounds, ending at the start of your turn 3 rounds later.

Daybreak

As a Bonus Action, you form a blade of solar flame. While Daybreak lasts, you gain these benefits:

  • Solar Flight. You have a Fly Speed of 40 feet and can hover, and you have Resistance to Fire damage.
  • Blade Projectiles. As a Magic action, you hurl two flaming blades, making a ranged spell attack for each. On a hit, a blade deals 3d10 Fire damage, and each creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes 2d6 Fire damage.
  • Blade Synergy. If you have Pact of the Blade, you can instead replace each attack in your Attack action with a Blade Projectile.

Well of Radiance

As a Magic action, you plant a sword of Light in the ground within 5 feet of you. When it appears, each creature of your choice within 15 feet of it makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. While the Well lasts, each ally within 15 feet of it gains these benefits:

  • It regains Hit Points equal to 1d8 plus your Charisma modifier at the start of each of its turns.
  • It has Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
  • It deals an extra 1d6 Radiant damage the first time it hits with an attack each turn.
Level 14

Sunfire

Both Supers. You no longer choose a Super when you finish a Long Rest. Each time you use your Super, choose Daybreak or Well of Radiance.

Ignition. When a Burning creature takes Fire damage from you, it Ignites (see Element Keywords in the Shared Chassis). Ignition is the Solar counterpart to Volatile, Jolt, and Unravel.

Icarus Dash. While you're in the air, once per turn you can fly up to 15 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks (no action required).
