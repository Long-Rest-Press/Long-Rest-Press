Orpheus Rig

Wondrous Item (Boots), Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic Armor

The tighter the snare, the more it gives back. A Hunter piece in Destiny.

Tethered Prey. Once per turn, when you hit a creature whose Speed is reduced or is 0, it takes an extra 1d6 Force damage.

Nightstalker Hook. If you have the Nightstalker's Deadfall Super, you regain one expended Grenade use for every two creatures your Deadfall tethers. Once per Long Rest, if a single Deadfall tethers five or more creatures, you regain your use of your Super when its anchor fades.

Very Rare · Exotic Armor

Mask of Bakris

Wondrous Item (Helm), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic Armor

An Eliksni-made mask. Its wearer is never quite where you aimed. A Hunter piece in Destiny. While wearing it, you gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class. It uses the Slowed, Frozen, and Jolted keywords from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

Light Shift. As a Bonus Action, you teleport up to 20 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Your next hit before the end of your turn deals an extra 2d6 damage if the target is Slowed, Frozen, or Jolted. You can use this property a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

Hunter Hook. If you have the Dodge Class Ability, your Dodge's movement becomes a teleport of up to 15 feet, and it doesn't expend uses of Light Shift.

Very Rare · Exotic

Le Monarque

Weapon (Longbow), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Poison Arrows. When you hit with this bow, the target takes an extra 1d6 Poison damage and must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or have the Poisoned condition until the end of its next turn.

Perfect Draw. The bow has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. If you haven't moved this turn, you can take a Bonus Action and expend 1 charge to fully draw the bow. Your next hit with it this turn ignores Half Cover and Three-Quarters Cover and releases a burst of toxin: the target and each creature of your choice within 10 feet of it make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 4d6 Poison damage and has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute; it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only.

Rare · Exotic

Leviathan's Breath

Weapon (Longbow), Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic

The Emperor's bow. It doesn't shoot arrows so much as launch them. In Destiny, Calus's massive Void bow knocks enemies back and staggers even the toughest foes. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Built for Strength. The bow has the Heavy Draw property, and its arrows deal 1d12 Piercing damage instead of 1d8. It's enormous: it keeps the Heavy property, but a creature with a Strength score below 13 has Disadvantage on attack rolls with it, even when using Dexterity (this overrides the normal Dexterity check for Heavy ranged weapons).

Unstoppable Arrow. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with this bow, the arrow detonates in a pulse of Void. The target takes an extra 1d8 Force damage, and if it's Large or smaller, it's pushed 15 feet away from you and can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn.

Very Rare · Exotic

Graviton Lance

Weapon (Lance), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic

It doesn't pierce the target. It collapses it. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon, and its damage can be Force instead of Piercing (your choice on each hit). Graviton Lance is a pulse rifle in Destiny; its Reach pairs with a gravity theme that pulls enemies into range.

Black Hole. Once per turn, when you hit with the lance, a second pulse of Void follows the first, and the target takes an extra 1d8 Force damage.

Cosmology. When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with the lance, it collapses into a small singularity. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of it becomes Volatile (see Element Keywords in the Destiny Overview & Chassis), so your next Force hit can set off a chain of explosions.

Singularity. The lance has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. When you hit with it, you can expend 1 charge to warp gravity around the target. The target and each creature of your choice within 15 feet of it make a DC 16 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is pulled up to 15 feet toward the target and has the Prone condition.

Very Rare · Exotic

Collective Obligation

Weapon (Quarterstaff), Very Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic

What the Disciple took, it now gives back. In Destiny, this Void pulse rifle from the Vow of the Disciple raid absorbed Void debuffs from enemies and loaded them into its own rounds. As a quarterstaff, its burst fire becomes a quick flurry of strikes, making it a natural Monk weapon. It uses the Volatile, Suppressed, and Weakened keywords from the Destiny Overview & Chassis. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Disciple's Mark. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with the staff, you can make it Weakened or Suppressed.

Void Leech. When you hit a creature that is Volatile, Suppressed, or Weakened, you can leech a copy of one of those conditions into the staff; the target keeps it. The staff holds one stored condition at a time. The next time you hit a different creature with the staff, you can unleash the stored condition on it.

Leech Burst. As a Bonus Action, you release the stored condition in a wave: each enemy within 10 feet of you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus your Proficiency Bonus plus your Dexterity or Wisdom modifier) or gain that condition. Once you use this property, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

Uncommon · Exotic

Telesto

Weapon (Sling), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

Famously the most unpredictable weapon in the system. In Destiny, Telesto fired sticky bolts that exploded a moment later, and it was infamous among players for its endless bugs. This sling honors both.

Overloaded Bolts. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with this sling, the bullet sticks and detonates at the start of your next turn: the target and each other creature within 5 feet of it take 1d6 Force damage. Each time a bullet detonates, roll a d6. On a 1, also roll on the Telesto Anomalies table.

d6Telesto Anomaly
1The bullet detonates twice.
2The bullet hops to a random creature within 30 feet of the target (ally or enemy) and detonates there instead.
3The detonation is a harmless burst of violet light, filling a 30-foot radius with dim light for 1 minute.
4"Telesto has been disabled." The sling can't be used until the end of your next turn.
5You're teleported 10 feet in a random direction to an unoccupied space.
6The detonation deals maximum damage.
Rare · Exotic

Black Talon

Weapon (Longsword), Rare (Requires Attunement), Exotic

Forged in memory of a fallen Awoken prince, by the Crows of the Black Hull. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

Crow's Wings. Once per turn, you can replace one attack with a slash that launches a crescent of Void. Make a ranged attack roll with the sword against a creature within 30 feet. On a hit, the target takes the sword's normal damage as Force damage and is Suppressed (see Element Keywords in the Destiny Overview & Chassis). If the target was already Suppressed, the effect also spreads to each creature of your choice within 10 feet of it.

Duelist's Trance. When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with the sword, you gain a +1 bonus to AC (maximum +2) until you sheathe the sword or finish a Short or Long Rest.

Uncommon · Exotic

Bad Juju

Weapon (Sickle), Uncommon (Requires Attunement), Exotic

The reaping gets easier as you go. In Destiny, this pulse rifle's String of Curses perk grew stronger with every kill. A sickle is the reaper's weapon.

String of Curses. When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with the sickle, it gains a Curse (maximum 3). The sickle deals 1 extra damage per Curse. All Curses fade when you finish a Short or Long Rest.
