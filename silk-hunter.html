Silk Hunter

A swift hunter who fights with a thread-bound needle, weaves Silk from every strike, crafts deadly tools, and changes her whole fighting style by the crest she wears.

v0.1 PlaytestRanger levels 3–15Inspired by Hollow Knight: Silksong
The Weavers are gone, but their silk still runs in a few bloodlines. Silk Hunters spin it from the rhythm of battle: every strike draws a strand, and enough strands bind wounds closed or lash out as spears and storms of thread. They carry crests, ancient emblems that reshape how a hunter moves and kills, and a satchel of tools built from scavenged shell and wire.

Level 3

Needle and Thread

Choose a Finesse melee weapon to be your needle. It gains the Thrown property (range 20/60) and returns to your hand on a silk thread after a thrown attack. When you hit a creature with a thrown needle, you can pull yourself up to 20 feet toward it, or pull a Medium or smaller target up to 10 feet toward you. The target can resist the pull with a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC.

Level 3

Silk

You weave power from the fight. Your Silk maximum is twice your Proficiency Bonus. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with your needle, you gain 1 Silk. You lose all Silk when you finish a Long Rest.

Level 3

Bind

As a Bonus Action, you can spend all your Silk (minimum 3) to bind your wounds. You regain 1d6 Hit Points per Silk spent, plus your Wisdom modifier. Your active crest can change how Bind works.

Level 3

Silk Skills

You know every Silk Skill below. At the end of each Long Rest, equip two of them; you equip three at Ranger level 11. Using an equipped Silk Skill costs the Silk shown and takes a Bonus Action unless noted. Silk Skills aren't spells, but they use your spell save DC.

Threadstorm2Threads lash out around you. Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of you makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d8 Slashing damage on a failed save or half as much on a success.

Creatures can see the runes and may flee.
Pale Nails3Three pale nails summon around you. At the start of your next turn, each one seeks a creature of your choice that you can see within 60 feet and hits it automatically for 1d8 Force damage. You can aim several nails at the same creature.
Level 3

Level 7

Tools

At the end of each Long Rest, you craft a number of tools from the list below equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of 1). An attack tool has a number of uses equal to your Proficiency Bonus; when you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a use of it. A passive tool has no uses and works while you carry it. Tools use your spell save DC.

You also carry one utility tool, or two at Ranger level 11, chosen at the end of each Long Rest. Utility tools help with exploration and don't count against the tools you craft.

Level 11

Needolin

You play your needle like a stringed instrument. As a Magic action, each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it has the Charmed condition until the end of its next turn. Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

Outside combat, playing for 1 minute in a place reveals one memory tied to it: the DM describes a scene from that place's past.

Level 11

Faydown Step

Once per turn, while you're in the air, you can jump again.

Level 15

Weaver's Legacy

The blood of the Weavers wakes in you.

Twin Crests. You can wear two crests at once, gaining both passives. When you Bind, choose which crest's Bind applies.

Silk Heart. When you roll Initiative, you gain Silk equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

Designer notes
  • Three action lanes. Silk Skills use the Bonus Action, tools replace an attack, and Bind is shaped by the crest. Silk Skills and Bind compete with Hunter's Mark for the Bonus Action.
  • Crests are the identity. Beast is the only crest with a real drawback, trading Silk Skills for damage and lifesteal. Twin Crests at level 15 opens combinations like Witch + Beast.
  • Silk carries between fights until a Long Rest, rewarding a hunter who ends a fight with Silk saved.
  • Concentration: Haste and Hunter's Mark both need it, so players choose between them.
Playtest questions
  • Is a Silk maximum of twice your Proficiency Bonus right for Bind's healing?
  • Is Pale Nails' 3 Silk cost right for 3d8 guaranteed damage?
  • More crests and tools, and possibly crest resonances like the Warrior of the Void's charms?
