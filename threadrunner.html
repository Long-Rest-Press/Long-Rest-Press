Threadrunner

Threadrunners weave the bonds between living things into rope, blade, and anchor. They never touch the ground for long.

v0.1 PlaytestDestiny Hunter · StrandInspired by Destiny 2
Download PDF
Shared Destiny rules →This subclass uses the Grenade, Charged Melee, Class Ability, Super, and element keyword rules from the Destiny Overview & Chassis.

The Threadrunner is the Strand Hunter as a Ranger subclass. It uses the Shared Chassis from the Destiny Overview & Chassis, with its Super at level 11 and the upgrade at level 15. Strand uses Psychic damage, reflecting its mind-over-matter nature. The key ability is Wisdom, so all saves and spell attacks use your spell save DC and spell attack modifier.

Level 3

Grapple

This is your Grenade. As a Bonus Action, you fire a Strand line at one of the following within 60 feet that you can see:

  • A surface or object. You pull yourself in a straight line to a space next to it, such as a wall, ceiling, ledge, or large object.
  • A creature. You pull yourself to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of it.

This movement doesn't use your Speed and doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks.

Grapple Strike. If you grappled to a creature, the next time you hit it with a melee attack before the end of your turn, it takes extra Psychic damage equal to your grenade damage.

Level 3

Threaded Spike

This is your Charged Melee. Once per turn, when you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a thrown Threaded Spike aimed at a creature within 30 feet. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes Psychic damage equal to your grenade damage.

The spike then ricochets to a second creature of your choice within 15 feet of the first. Make a new attack roll against it, dealing the same damage on a hit. The spike then returns to your hand.

Level 7

Widow's Silk

Endless Lines. Once on each of your turns, you can use Grapple without expending a use. You expend a use only when you want to add Grapple Strike damage.

Woven Mail. When you hit with Grapple Strike or Threaded Spike, you gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage until the start of your next turn.

Level 7

Dodge

You gain the Dodge Class Ability from the Shared Chassis. Threadrunner twist: when you Dodge, you can use Grapple instead of moving up to half your Speed, without expending a use.

Level 11

Super: Silkstrike

You can use Silkstrike once per Long Rest. You can regain its use by expending a level 3+ spell slot (no action required).

As a Bonus Action, you weave a rope dart staff that lasts for 3 rounds, ending at the start of your turn 3 rounds later. While it lasts, you gain these benefits:

  • Rope Dart. The rope dart is a melee weapon with 15-foot reach that you're proficient with. It deals 1d10 Psychic damage plus your choice of your Wisdom or Dexterity modifier.
  • Lunge. Before each attack with the rope dart, you can move up to 20 feet toward the target. This movement doesn't use your Speed and doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks.
  • Whirl. Once per turn, when you hit with the rope dart, each other creature of your choice within 10 feet of you takes 2d8 Psychic damage.
  • Free Lines. Using Grapple requires no action, though you can still use it only once per turn.
Level 15

Unravel

Unraveled. When a creature takes damage from your Grapple Strike, Threaded Spike, or Silkstrike, it becomes Unraveled until the end of your next turn. The next time an Unraveled creature takes damage from you, threads lash out from it: up to two creatures of your choice within 10 feet of it each take 2d6 Psychic damage. The creature then stops being Unraveled.

Whirling Maelstrom. Silkstrike's Whirl now reaches creatures within 15 feet of you, and Lunge's movement increases to 30 feet.
