Welcome to the Long Rest Press Blog

Hey, I'm Tom, and I make everything you find on Long Rest Press.

If you've been here before, you know how it works: new D&D 2024 subclasses twice a week, most of them pulled from video games I can't stop thinking about. Void-wielding monks, a ranger who hunts with silk and needle, sorcerers running on red magic. Every so often an adventure, a dungeon, or a new game mechanic slips into the rotation too.

What the site hasn't had is a place where I can just talk to you. That's what this is.

What you'll find here

  • What's coming up. I release content in runs now: three or four posts from the same game in a row. Before each run starts, I'll post a heads-up so you know what's coming.
  • What I'm working on. Some projects take a lot longer than a subclass. Right now that includes a roguelike siege mode for D&D 2024 and Draw Steel, where you spend the day rebuilding your town and the night holding the walls. I'll share where those projects stand, including the parts that aren't working yet.
  • Designer's notes. Why a feature works the way it does, what I cut, and what changed after it hit the table.
  • Your requests. I reorder releases based on what people ask for. If there's a class you want covered or a game you want to see, tell me.

Where else to find me

I'm also starting a YouTube channel with an overview video for each subclass: what it does, how it plays, and what kind of player will love it. youtube.com/@LongRestPress

That's it for now. Pull up a chair. First up on the blog: we're heading to Hallownest.
