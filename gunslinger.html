Gunslinger

Gunslingers are the Light's sharpshooters. Every throw is a trick shot, and the crowd is the target.

v0.1 PlaytestHunter · SolarInspired by Destiny 2
Shared Destiny rules →This subclass uses the Grenade, Charged Melee, Class Ability, Super, and element keyword rules from the Destiny Overview & Chassis, including the Rogue Dodge rule and the Burning and Ignite keywords.

The Gunslinger is the Solar Hunter as a Rogue subclass. Like the Revenant, it gains Dodge at level 9, its Super at an added level 10 feature, and the Super upgrade at 13. Destiny's Golden Gun is reimagined as the Golden Bow, keeping three devastating precision shots without a firearm. Solar uses Fire damage. The key ability is Dexterity, so your save DC is 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Dexterity modifier.

Level 3

Tripmine Grenade

This is your Grenade. As a Bonus Action, you stick a Solar mine to a surface you can see within 60 feet. The mine lasts for 1 minute, and you can have only one at a time. When a creature you can see enters a space within 10 feet of the mine, you can detonate it (no action required). Each creature of your choice within 10 feet of the mine makes a Dexterity saving throw.

  • Failed save: The creature takes your grenade damage as Fire damage and is Burning.
  • Successful save: The creature takes half as much damage only.
Level 3

Knife Trick

This is your Charged Melee. Once per turn, when you take the Attack action, you can replace your attack with a fan of three Solar knives conjured from Light. Choose up to three different creatures within 30 feet, and make a ranged attack roll using your Dexterity modifier against each. The knives count as Finesse weapons, but only one hit per turn can deal Sneak Attack damage. Each hit deals Fire damage equal to half your grenade damage, and the target is Burning. The knives vanish after the attacks, so you never carry or lose them.

Level 3

Practiced Ignition

When you deal Sneak Attack damage to a Burning creature, it Ignites.

Level 9

Dodge and Knock 'Em Down

Dodge. You gain the Dodge Class Ability through the Rogue Dodge rule in the Shared Chassis. Gunslinger twist (Gambler's Dodge): if you Dodge while within 5 feet of an enemy, you can immediately use Knife Trick again (no action required), even if you've already used it this turn.

Knock 'Em Down. When your Sneak Attack reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, you regain one expended use of your Grenade. Once you regain a use this way, you can't do so again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

Level 10

Super

When you finish a Long Rest, choose Golden Bow or Blade Barrage as your Super. You can regain its use by expending two uses of your Grenade (no action required).

Golden Bow

As a Bonus Action, you conjure a longbow of solid Light loaded with three Golden Shots. You're proficient with the bow. It lasts for 3 rounds, ending at the start of your turn 3 rounds later, or earlier if you have no Golden Shots left.

  • Golden Shot. Once on each of your turns, you can replace your attack with a Golden Shot, and you can also fire one as a Bonus Action. A Golden Shot is a ranged attack with a range of 120/360 feet. On a hit, it deals 5d10 Fire damage, can deal Sneak Attack damage, and makes the target Burning.

Blade Barrage

As an action, you leap and hurl a volley of explosive Solar knives. Choose up to five creatures within 60 feet. Each makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 4d10 Fire damage and is Burning. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage only. On your next turn, you can take an action to throw a second volley in the same way.

Level 13

Showstopper

Both Supers. You no longer choose a Super when you finish a Long Rest. Each time you use your Super, choose Golden Bow or Blade Barrage.

Golden Precision. When a Golden Shot reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, the bow regains that Golden Shot. Regained shots let you fire more than three per activation: a fourth shot after one kill, and so on, up to a maximum of six Golden Shots fired per activation.

Burning Barrage. Each creature that fails its saving throw against Blade Barrage also Ignites.

Level 17

Celestial Nighthawk

Once per Long Rest, when you activate Golden Bow, you can load it with a single Celestial shot instead of three Golden Shots. That shot deals 15d10 Fire damage on a hit, and if you apply Sneak Attack to it, the Sneak Attack dice deal maximum damage. The target then Ignites. Golden Precision can't regain a Celestial shot.
