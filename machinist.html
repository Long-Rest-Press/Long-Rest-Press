Machinist

Machinists run hot. Every shot builds Heat, which they spend on gadgets or unleash in a rapid-fire Hypercharge, at the risk of overheating.

v0.1 PlaytestArtificer levels 3–15Inspired by Final Fantasy
Download PDF

Inspired by the Machinist of Final Fantasy XIV and Edgar's Tools from Final Fantasy VI, the gun is the heart of this subclass: build, spend, overheat, repeat.

Level 3

Tools of the Trade

You gain proficiency with Tinker's Tools and with firearms (such as the Pistol and Musket from the Dungeon Master's Guide). You can use a firearm you're proficient with as a Spellcasting Focus for your Artificer spells.

Level 3

Machinist Spells

When you reach an Artificer level specified in the table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

Artificer LevelSpells
3Catapult (legacy), Thunderwave
5Heat Metal, Shatter
9Haste, Lightning Bolt
13Arcane Eye, Fire Shield
17Animate Objects, Telekinesis
Level 3

Heat

Each time you hit a creature with a firearm attack, you gain 1 Heat, to a maximum of 5. Your Heat drops to 0 when you finish a Short or Long Rest, or when 1 minute passes without you making an attack.

You can spend Heat on the following gadgets. Using a gadget replaces one attack when you take the Attack action, except Drill, which you use as a Bonus Action. Gadgets that call for a saving throw use your spell save DC.

GadgetHeatEffect
Drill1Bonus Action. Your next hit with a firearm this turn ignores Resistance and deals an extra 1d8 damage.
AutoCrossbow3Make one firearm attack against each of up to three creatures you can see within 30 feet of you.
Bio Blaster2Each creature in a 15-foot Cone makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 2d6 Poison damage and having the Poisoned condition until the end of your next turn on a failure, or taking half damage on a success.
Level 3

Hypercharge

When you have 5 Heat, you can spend all of it as a Bonus Action. Until the end of your next turn, you can make one extra firearm attack whenever you take the Attack action, and your firearm hits deal an extra 1d6 Fire damage. When Hypercharge ends, you are Overheated: you can't gain Heat until the end of your following turn.

Level 5

Extra Attack

You can attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

Level 9

Advanced Gadgets

Your maximum Heat increases to 7, though Hypercharge still requires and spends 5 Heat. You also gain the following gadgets:

GadgetHeatEffect
NoiseBlaster2One creature you can see within 30 feet makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it can't take Reactions and has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.
Chainsaw3Make a melee attack against a creature within 5 feet, using your Intelligence modifier for the attack roll. On a hit, it takes 3d10 Slashing damage. If it has 30 Hit Points or fewer after taking this damage, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or drop to 0 Hit Points.
Level 15

Wildfire

As a Bonus Action, you mark a creature you can see within 60 feet of you. Until the end of your next turn, count each time you hit it with a firearm attack. When the effect ends, the mark detonates, dealing 2d10 Fire damage to the creature for each hit counted.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

Playtest notes
  • Heat pacing: the target is Hypercharge roughly every 2–3 turns with Extra Attack. Check whether misses slow it down too much at low levels.
  • Gadgets vs. Hypercharge: see whether players ever spend Heat on gadgets, or always save for Hypercharge.
  • AutoCrossbow: now 3 Heat for up to three targets. Check that it's worth using against spread-out groups without outshining Hypercharge.
  • Chainsaw: the 30 HP threshold at 9th level may be too generous or too stingy. Compare it with the Sniper's Death Sickle.
  • Wildfire + Hypercharge: both in one turn could stack 5–6 hits for 10–12d10 extra damage. Confirm a once-per-Long-Rest nuke at 15th feels right.
  • Firearms: confirm your table uses the DMG's firearm rules.
  • Hypercharge snowball: Heat can still be gained while Hypercharge is active, so a strong Hypercharge can rebuild most of the next one before Overheat. Watch whether this chains too often.
