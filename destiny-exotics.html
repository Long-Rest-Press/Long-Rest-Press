Destiny Exotics Overview

Destiny's Exotic weapons and armor, translated into D&D 2024 magic items across every rarity tier.

Framework

Every Destiny Exotic becomes a magic item whose rarity matches its power, so the list spans a whole campaign rather than sitting at the top tier.

RarityWhat it gets
UncommonA single quirky Destiny perk, no attack bonus
Rare+1, plus one signature perk
Very Rare+1 or +2, a signature perk, and a charged active
Legendary+2 or +3, plus an active that defines the fight

Optional Rule: Exotic. In Destiny you can equip only one Exotic weapon and one Exotic armor piece at a time. Under this rule, a creature can be attuned to only one Exotic weapon and one Exotic armor piece at once. Every item in these drops is Exotic unless its entry says Non-Exotic.

New Weapon Property: Heavy Draw. When you make an attack with a ranged weapon that has this property, you use your choice of your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls; you must use the same modifier for both rolls. Heavy Draw is the mirror of Finesse, letting strong characters use a bow without relying on Dexterity.

Ahamkara Items

The Ahamkara were wish-dragons, hunted to extinction because their bargains always cost too much. Their bones were woven into armor afterward. Warlocks understood the risk; Hunters and Titans mostly didn't. Every Ahamkara item (including One Thousand Voices) shares these rules.

Whispers of the Ahamkara. The bones talk. In quiet moments they flatter you, ask what you want, and offer to help, always addressing you with fond, possessive endearments. The DM plays the voice: charming, generous, and never quite trustworthy.

Boon. Once per Long Rest, when you fail a saving throw, miss with an attack roll, or fail an ability check, the voice offers its help. If you accept, you succeed instead, and the DM gains one Debt.

Debts. The DM can call in a Debt at any time to impose a price: Disadvantage on one important roll, a sudden temptation the character must resist with a Wisdom saving throw, a demand from the voice the character would rather not meet, or a complication in the story. A called Debt can't be refused; the only question is what it costs.
