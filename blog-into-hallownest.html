The Next Two Weeks: Into Hallownest

Get ready for bugs, blades, and a kingdom that fell a long time ago. For the next two weeks, every release on Long Rest Press comes from Hollow Knight.

You may have met two of them already. Warrior of the Void gave monks the hollow, silent power of the Vessel, and the Silk Hunter turned rangers into needle-and-thread predators. Now the rest of the kingdom gets its turn.

What's coming

  • College of the Nightmare Troupe - Bard. A wandering troupe of performers whose show is lit by a nightmare flame. Every performance has a price, and the troupe always collects.
  • Nailmaster - Fighter. For the warrior who has spent a lifetime perfecting a few signature strikes, and can end a fight with one of them.
  • School of the Soul Sanctum - Wizard. A scholar who harvests Soul from the dying and risks the same fate as the Sanctum's Master.
  • Path of the Hive - Barbarian. A relentless guardian who fights in a storm of bees and heals off the hive's royal blood.

Why these four

When I do a run, I try to spread it across as many classes as I can, so there's something here whether you play a support caster, a frontliner, or someone who'd rather blink around and let their experiments loose on the world. Hallownest made that easy. Almost every corner of it has a fighting style worth borrowing from.

And after that

The Warrior of the Void and the Silk Hunter aren't finished. I have more charms and more hunter's tools for both, and they'll arrive later as an update instead of crowding the original posts. Additionally, there are several more character-based subclasses coming in the future.

If there's a Hollow Knight or Silksong character you want to see as a subclass, let me know. The kingdoms have plenty more inspiration to give.
